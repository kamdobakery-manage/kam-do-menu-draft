故事呼應

HEAD ↔ TAIL · 首尾呼應

開頭故事 · 定稿

金都的故事，從 1986 年的香港說起 —— 一間街角茶餐廳，一鑊熱氣，一份港式人情。

1993 年，我們帶著同一份手藝跨過太平洋，在溫哥華照舊開起茶餐廳。後來，招牌的菠蘿包與蛋塔越做越旺，金都漸漸成了街坊熟知的餅店。

走得再遠，也不忘來時的路。如今返璞歸真，做回那間有鑊氣、有人情味的港式茶餐廳。

金都，味道如昔。

The Kam Do story begins in Hong Kong, 1986 — a corner cha chaan teng, a hot wok, and a warmth you could taste.

In 1993, we carried the same craft across the Pacific and opened our doors in Vancouver, a cha chaan teng once again. In time, our pineapple buns and egg tarts grew so beloved that, to many, Kam Do became a bakery.

But however far you travel, you never forget where you began. Today we return to our roots — back to the wok-fire, the warmth, and the cha chaan teng that started it all.

Kam Do — still the taste you remember.
